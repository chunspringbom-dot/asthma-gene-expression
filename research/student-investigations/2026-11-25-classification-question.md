# How much does an asthma diagnosis tell us about airway inflammation?

On November 25, the student asked how well a disease label captures differences between patients, whether symptoms reveal those differences, and whether to investigate subgroup frequencies or mechanisms. The narrower question below is an AI-assisted recommendation pending student selection.

## Candidate question

Among patients with asthma in GSE89809, how closely does the Asthma Control Questionnaire (ACQ) score track a predefined airway epithelial type 2 expression score? How much variation in expression is visible among patients with similar asthma control?

Individual epithelial records include ACQ across [mild](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2389887), [moderate](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2389919), and [severe](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2389951) asthma. A [previous reanalysis](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12920-021-00892-4) already related coexpression modules to clinical variables including ACQ. A [multicohort study](https://doi.org/10.1165/rcmb.2024-0125OC) examined eight public epithelial datasets. Novelty of the exact score comparison remains unverified.

Before calculating results, audit unique patients, tissue, missing values, corticosteroid exposure and the ACQ version. Fix the score and analysis method in advance. Plot one point per patient and estimate the association with uncertainty. These measurements can address concordance within this cohort. Testing clinician judgement requires recorded clinician predictions. Estimating population prevalence or identifying causal mechanisms requires a different design.

No original expression analysis has been run for this question.
