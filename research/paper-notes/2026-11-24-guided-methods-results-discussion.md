## Assistant-supported Discussion review

Source: [Christenson et al. 2015, Discussion and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407484/), indexed primary text retrieved Nov 24; direct full-text opening encountered browser verification. [Abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25611785/).

Authors' interpretation: asthma-related airway expression patterns characterize a candidate COPD endotype; asthma history does not adequately identify the molecular subgroup; the signature has potential as a treatment-response marker, not established clinical utility. Strengths include cross-cohort evidence and a randomized placebo-controlled treatment cohort. Authors acknowledge uncertain subgroup size, limited phenotypic characterization and cross-dataset comparability. Endpoint-specific treatment findings require further validation. A predictive biomarker requires evidence of differential treatment effect, not merely outcome association.
