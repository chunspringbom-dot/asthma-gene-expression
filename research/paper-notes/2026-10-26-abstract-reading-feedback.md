## C. Revised learning method — agreed direction

**Old assumption:** Student independently reads a long technical paper, immediately fills out all sections of the Markdown notebook and proceeds along the original roadmap.

**Problem observed:** Student has read only part of the Abstract; statistical and biomedical terminology, complex multi-cohort design, constrained computer access, and SAT preparation make that plan unrealistic for now.

**New workflow:** (1) Assistant explains essential concepts and the paper's map (**clearly labeled as external assistance**) → (2) student reads *one small relevant section* and locates/verifies each claim → (3) student writes their own plain-English understanding and any objections → (4) checks citations and commits their own notes. Focus first on the **asthma dataset and its methods**, not every COPD treatment detail.

**Verification test before each next section:** Student can answer one conceptual question from memory and link it to an original source location.
