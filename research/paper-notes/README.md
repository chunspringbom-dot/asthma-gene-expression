# Paper Reading Notes

This folder records my own reading of scientific papers used in this project. The goal is not to copy an abstract or produce a polished summary immediately; it is to preserve what I understood, what confused me, what evidence supports the authors' claims, and how my understanding changes after checking the paper.

## How to use this space

1. Read a small section of the paper.
2. Write my understanding in my own words before looking up explanations.
3. Record the paper's exact evidence separately from my interpretation or questions.
4. Mark uncertain statements as **Unclear** rather than guessing.
5. Add page, section, figure, table, or paragraph details so claims can be checked later.
6. Update the notes after resolving a question; preserve important changes in understanding.

Use the [paper-reading note template](../../templates/paper-reading-note-template.md) for future papers.

## Annotation labels

- **[MY UNDERSTANDING]** — my own explanation in plain language.
- **[PAPER EVIDENCE]** — what the authors actually report, with a location in the paper.
- **[QUESTION]** — something I do not understand yet.
- **[CHECKED]** — a question I investigated and resolved, with the source used.
- **[CRITIQUE]** — limitations, alternative explanations, or possible weaknesses.
- **[PROJECT CONNECTION]** — how this paper may affect my research question or analysis.

## Research integrity

These notes are a learning record, not a substitute for the original paper. Keep direct quotations short and clearly marked, cite the section/page/figure, and distinguish the authors' conclusions from my own inferences. Do not describe a question as answered unless I have checked the evidence.
