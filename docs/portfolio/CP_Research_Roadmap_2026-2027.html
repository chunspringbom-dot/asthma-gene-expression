03 / Scientific direction and boundaries

Provisional learning question: In GSE67472, which genes differ between asthma and healthy-control airway epithelial samples, and how do selected inflammation-related patterns compare with the original study?

The project record identifies GSE67472 as the asthma/control candidate. The paper's COPD cohorts and treatment study are separate. The candidate must not be treated as a COPD treatment-response dataset.

  1. Understand the paper: finish the relevant Methods and connect them to the research rationale and key results. Check any remaining Introduction gaps; do not mark the full section complete automatically.
  2. Learn the dataset and workflow: inspect metadata, processing, groups, and analysis suitability; perform a labeled educational reproduction only after verification.
  3. Compare 5–10 relevant papers: record question, data, method, findings, and limitations.
  4. Evaluate a possible gap: distinguish an unanswered question from something the student has simply not read yet.
  5. Finalize a feasible question: specify comparison, outcomes, exclusions, and methods before the main analysis.
  6. Analyze and check: retain outputs, settings, source links, and sensitivity checks justified by the design.
  7. Write the English report: separate published findings, educational reproduction, and any independent extension.

Minimum successful outcome: an honest educational reanalysis with reproducible methods and limitations. A novel contribution or independent-cohort validation is an extension, not a promised result.
