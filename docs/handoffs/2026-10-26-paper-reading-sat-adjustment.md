## 1. Source of truth: what is paper, data, and student research?

### Publication
Christenson SA et al. 2015. **Asthma-COPD overlap. Clinical relevance of genomic signatures of type 2 inflammation in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.** *Am J Respir Crit Care Med* 191(7):758–766. DOI [10.1164/rccm.201408-1458OC](https://doi.org/10.1164/rccm.201408-1458OC). [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25611785/) · [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407484/).

**Why published researchers asked the question:** COPD is heterogeneous; some patients may express biological patterns similar to asthma. Could asthma-associated airway epithelial gene-expression signatures also be observed in some COPD patients, alongside asthma-like disease features and certain corticosteroid responses?

### Paper design — orientation for the next session, not student-authored findings

1. **Asthma derivation dataset:** airway epithelial brushings from **62 subjects with mild-to-moderate asthma not using inhaled steroids + 43 healthy controls = 105**; asthma subjects stratified into Th2-high/Th2-low using POSTN, SERPINB2 and CLCA1 (TGM).
2. **COPD comparison cohorts:** BAEC **n=237** and SAEC **n=171** provide independent airway expression data to examine whether asthma-related gene sets appear in COPD-related patterns. Different platforms/participants led authors to compare **gene rankings via GSEA** instead of pooling data as one simple cohort.
3. **Th2 signature development:** **TGM** means scaled **three-gene mean** (POSTN/SERPINB2/CLCA1). **T2S** refers to the broader **100-gene** Th2-related metric; these measures are **not interchangeable**. See full text **Statistical Analysis → Th2 gene signature scores**.
4. **COPD clinical and treatment association:** separate **GLUCOLD** study (n=89 in the abstract, analysis-specific numbers may differ), a **randomized placebo-controlled** study, examined gene-signature relation to tissue/blood eosinophils, bronchodilator reversibility and treatment outcomes.
5. **Results claimed by the publication (not generated by this student):** asthma-related gene sets showed enrichment among COPD-associated expression patterns; higher T2S associated with reduced lung-function measures but not asthma history, along with certain asthma-like features. In GLUCOLD, higher baseline T2S was associated with **improvement in hyperinflation** on inhaled corticosteroid treatment compared with placebo; **not all endpoints** showed this treatment association.
6. **Discussion and limitations:** Type 2 inflammatory processes may characterize a COPD subset. Observed signatures are **associations**, not proof that gene expression causes disease or a validated clinical prediction tool; subgroup size and detailed phenotype require stronger evidence.

**Source sections:** [Abstract → Rationale/Objectives/Methods/Main Results/Conclusions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25611785/); [full-text Methods, Table 1, Figures 1–5, Results, Discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407484/).

### Important boundary: GSE67472 is NOT the whole paper

[**GSE67472 — GEO official accession**](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE67472) is the **105 asthma/healthy airway epithelial samples** from the broader research. The series documents the asthma vs healthy and Th2-high vs Th2-low/healthy expression comparisons. It does **not** include the paper's BAEC, SAEC or GLUCOLD treatment outcomes as its own samples. Its Affymetrix microarray platform is GPL16311.

**Our provisional capstone question:** Which genes differ between asthma and healthy airway epithelial samples in GSE67472, and how does a limited, source-based inflammation-related pattern compare with the original study?

**Not our claim:** a new clinical treatment predictor, an independently replicated experiment, causal proof, or new COPD-cohort measurements. Dataset/contrast still await proper sample mapping and feasibility checks.

Longer source-linked reading reference: [Christenson 2015 paper structure guide](../../research/literature/christenson-2015-paper-structure.md).
