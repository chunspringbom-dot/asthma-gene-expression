## 4. Evidence and sources

- **NCBI GEO GSE67472:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE67472
  - The record identifies the study as “Airway epithelial gene expression in asthma versus healthy controls.”
  - It reports microarray analysis of airway epithelial brushings from 62 people with mild-to-moderate asthma not on inhaled steroids and 43 healthy controls.
  - It states that asthma samples were stratified into Th2-high and Th2-low groups based on POSTN, SERPINB2, and CLCA1.
  - This supports the dataset's apparent topical fit, but not yet the final dataset decision or the feasibility of every proposed comparison.
