# Research Direction Decision — Reproduction Before Independent Research

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Status:** Adopted as a research strategy; final research question and dataset remain provisional.  
**Authorship:** Assistant-drafted synthesis of the student's questions and decisions; not a student-written manuscript or completed analysis.

## Why this decision was needed

The student questioned (1) whether comparing asthma and healthy airway epithelial expression in GSE67472 is scientifically meaningful, (2) whether published studies may reanalyse earlier work, (3) whether university researchers' superior validation makes a student replication redundant, and (4) whether reproducing a published study is effectively copying it.

These questions changed how the project should describe its scientific contribution.

## Long-term objective

**Working objective:** Critically evaluate the reproducibility, methodological sensitivity, and limitations of published asthma-related airway epithelial gene-expression findings, then formulate a feasible independent question grounded in a documented literature gap.

This is an aspiration, **not** a claim that an original contribution, independent cohort, or publishable result has been identified.

## Three-phase strategy

### Phase 1 — Learn and reproduce

- Read the Christenson et al. (2015) paper carefully, distinguishing the asthma GSE67472 series from COPD cohorts and the GLUCOLD treatment analysis.
- Audit GSE67472 metadata, sample groups, platform, and preprocessing before any comparison.
- If feasible, perform a documented exploratory asthma-versus-healthy differential-expression analysis.
- Learn log fold change, multiple-testing-adjusted p-values, effect sizes, and interpretation.
- Label this an **educational reproduction/reanalysis**, not a novel biomarker discovery.
- Reproduction within the same dataset is **not** independent external validation.

### Phase 2 — Map the existing literature

- Start with approximately 5–10 relevant original and review papers, expanding as needed.
- Record for each: research question, cohort/dataset, groups, methods, major findings, limitations, and overlap with our proposed analysis.
- Check which GSE67472 analyses and independent validations have already been published.
- Distinguish literature review, statistical meta-analysis, and secondary analysis of raw/processed public data.
- Identify a genuinely unanswered and feasible question **before** claiming originality.

### Phase 3 — Test a distinct question if feasible

Potential directions to evaluate, **not yet selected**:
- Robustness of selected findings to defensible preprocessing or statistical choices.
- Consistency of pre-specified expression patterns in an appropriately comparable independent cohort.
- Interpretation of discrepancies in relation to cohort composition, treatment, tissue, platform, or measurement.

An independent cohort must be demonstrably distinct; shared samples do not qualify. The feasibility of clinical metadata and cross-platform comparisons must be checked first. If no defensible research gap or dataset exists, complete an honest, reproducible educational study instead of manufacturing novelty.

## Research integrity principles

1. **Replication is not plagiarism:** reuse of published methods/data can be legitimate with attribution, accurate provenance, and transparent description of what was reproduced. Copying text, figures, or others' results as one's own is not.
2. **A matching result is not automatically a novel finding:** report both agreements and disagreements with prior work, and investigate possible reasons.
3. **Association is not causation:** expression differences and T2S associations do not establish disease mechanisms or treatment effects.
4. **Direction alone is not statistical replication:** assess effect sizes, uncertainty, adjusted p-values, sample size, and comparability.
5. **Nonsignificant is not proof of no effect:** interpret uncertainty rather than labeling studies simply correct or incorrect.
6. **Avoid clinical overclaiming:** do not infer treatment-response prediction from GSE67472 alone.
7. **Maintain authorship transparency:** distinguish student interpretation, assistant-supported synthesis, source facts, and analysis output.
8. **Do not claim a result until executed and verified.**
