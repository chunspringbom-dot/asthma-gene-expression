# Decision on November 25, 2026

The student selected the following direction after asking how much a shared diagnosis tells us about individual patients.

Among patients with asthma, how closely does asthma control (ACQ) track a predefined type 2 gene-expression score in airway epithelial cells? How much does the expression score vary among patients with similar ACQ scores?

The earlier GSE67472 asthma–control comparison addressed differences between diagnostic groups. The Q9 subgroup and FEV₁ hypotheses introduced separate outcomes, and patient-level FEV₁ linkage was unresolved. The new question compares two measurements from the same person. GSE89809 is the leading candidate because individual epithelial sample records include ACQ.

A ranking across diseases would require comparable tissues and outcome scales. Assessing clinician judgement would require recorded predictions. A cross-sectional sample can describe within-cohort variation while leaving causes and population frequencies open. Christenson et al. and the Q9 work supply background. The selected question is a capstone reanalysis with an open novelty assessment.
